# Email reply

Answer each question in the email. Keep the reply under five sentences. Thank the sender in the first line.

## Steps

1. Thank the sender for one specific thing.
2. Answer each question, in the order that the sender asked them.
3. Say what happens next, and when.
4. Sign with your first name.

Use [the openers](references/openers.md) when you are not sure how to start.
